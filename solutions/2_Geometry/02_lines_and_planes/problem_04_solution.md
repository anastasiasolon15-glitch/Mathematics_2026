# Exercise 4: Plane Through Three Points

Two vectors in the plane are

$$
\vec{AB}=(-1,1,0),\qquad \vec{AC}=(-1,0,1).
$$

Their cross product is

$$
\vec{AB}\times\vec{AC}
=
\begin{vmatrix}
i&j&k\\
-1&1&0\\
-1&0&1
\end{vmatrix}
=(1,1,1).
$$

Using $A=(1,0,0)$, the plane is

$$
(x-1)+y+z=0,
$$

or

$$
x+y+z=1.
$$

Checks:

$$
A:1+0+0=1,\quad B:0+1+0=1,\quad C:0+0+1=1.
$$
