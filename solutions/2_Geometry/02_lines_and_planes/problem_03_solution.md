# Exercise 3: Plane Through a Point with a Given Normal Vector

Using $n=(2,1,-1)$, the plane through $P=(1,-2,3)$ is

$$
n\cdot((x,y,z)-P)=0.
$$

Hence

$$
2(x-1)+(y+2)-(z-3)=0
$$

and, after simplifying,

$$
2x+y-z+3=0.
$$

Substitution gives

$$
2(1)+(-2)-3+3=0,
$$

so $P$ lies in the plane. The normal vector is perpendicular to every direction vector in the plane and determines the plane's orientation.
