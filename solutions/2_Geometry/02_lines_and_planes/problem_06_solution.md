# Exercise 6: Angle Between Planes

The normal vectors are

$$
n_1=(1,1,1),\qquad n_2=(1,-1,0).
$$

They are not scalar multiples, so the planes are not parallel.

The acute angle $\theta$ satisfies

$$
\cos\theta=
\frac{|n_1\cdot n_2|}{\lVert n_1\rVert\lVert n_2\rVert}.
$$

Since

$$
n_1\cdot n_2=1-1+0=0,
$$

we get $\cos\theta=0$, hence

$$
\theta=90^\circ.
$$

Normals alone suffice because the angle between planes equals the angle between their normals.
