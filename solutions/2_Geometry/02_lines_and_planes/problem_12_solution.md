# Exercise 12: Intersection Line of Two Planes

The equations are

$$
x+y+z=3,\qquad x-y+z=1.
$$

Subtracting the second from the first gives $2y=2$, so $y=1$. Then $x+z=2$.

Let $z=t$. Then $x=2-t$, giving

$$
(x,y,z)=(2,1,0)+t(-1,0,1).
$$

The plane normals are

$$
n_1=(1,1,1),\qquad n_2=(1,-1,1).
$$

Their cross product is

$$
n_1\times n_2=(2,0,-2),
$$

which is a scalar multiple of $(1,0,-1)$, and hence also of $(-1,0,1)$. Thus both methods give the same direction, with opposite scalar choices.
