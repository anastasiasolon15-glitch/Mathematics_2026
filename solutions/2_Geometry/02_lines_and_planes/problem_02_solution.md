# Exercise 2: A Line in Space

The point $(2,-1,3)$ lies on the line, and a direction vector is $v=(1,2,-2)$.

For $P=(4,3,-1)$, solve

$$
(2,-1,3)+t(1,2,-2)=(4,3,-1).
$$

The coordinate equations are

$$
2+t=4,\quad -1+2t=3,\quad 3-2t=-1,
$$

which all give $t=2$. Therefore $P$ lies on $L$.

For $t=-1$, the point is

$$
(2,-1,3)-(1,2,-2)=(1,-3,5).
$$
