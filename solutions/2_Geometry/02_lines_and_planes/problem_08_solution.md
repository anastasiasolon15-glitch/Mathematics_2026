# Exercise 8: Intersection Point of a Line and a Plane

The line is

$$
(x,y,z)=(1+t,2t,2-t).
$$

Substitute into $x+y+z=4$:

$$
(1+t)+2t+(2-t)=4.
$$

Hence

$$
3+2t=4,\qquad t=\frac12.
$$

The intersection point is

$$
\left(1+\frac12,\,2\cdot\frac12,\,2-\frac12\right)
=\left(\frac32,1,\frac32\right).
$$

Check:

$$
\frac32+1+\frac32=4.
$$
