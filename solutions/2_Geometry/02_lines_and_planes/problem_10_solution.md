# Exercise 10: Projection of a Point onto a Plane

The plane $x+y+z=3$ has normal $n=(1,1,1)$. The perpendicular line through $P=(2,1,3)$ is

$$
(x,y,z)=P+t(1,1,1).
$$

Substituting gives

$$
(2+t)+(1+t)+(3+t)=3,
$$

so $6+3t=3$, hence $t=-1$. The projection is

$$
P_\Pi=(2,1,3)-(1,1,1)=(1,0,2).
$$

The reflection is on the opposite side at the same distance:

$$
P'=P+2(-1)n=(2,1,3)-2(1,1,1)=(0,-1,1).
$$

The midpoint is

$$
\frac{P+P'}2=(1,0,2),
$$

and $1+0+2=3$, so it lies on the plane.
