# Exercise 9: Distances

## Point to a line

For $ax+by+c=0$,

$$
d=\frac{|ax_0+by_0+c|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}.
$$

Therefore

$$
d=\frac{|3(2)+4(3)-10|}{\sqrt{3^2+4^2}}
=\frac{8}{5}.
$$

## Point to a plane

For $ax+by+cz+d=0$,

$$
d=\frac{|ax_0+by_0+cz_0+d|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}}.
$$

Therefore

$$
d=\frac{|2(2)-2(-1)+3-4|}
{\sqrt{2^2+(-2)^2+1^2}}
=\frac{5}{3}.
$$

Both formulas use a normal: the numerator is the absolute value of the signed equation, and the denominator is the normal-vector length.
