# Exercise 5: Relative Position of Two Lines

The direction vectors are

$$
v_1=(1,2,1),\qquad v_2=(0,1,-1).
$$

They are not scalar multiples, so the lines are not parallel.

For an intersection, solve

$$
(1,1,0)+t(1,2,1)
=(2,0,1)+s(0,1,-1).
$$

The coordinate equations are

$$
1+t=2,\qquad 1+2t=s,\qquad t=1-s.
$$

The first gives $t=1$, and the second gives $s=3$, but the third becomes $1=-2$, a contradiction. Therefore the lines do not intersect.

Because they are neither parallel nor intersecting, they are **skew**.
