# Exercise 1: Line Through Two Points

The direction vector is

$$
\vec{AB}=B-A=(4-1,-1-2)=(3,-3).
$$

A parametrization is

$$
(x,y)=(1,2)+t(3,-3).
$$

Thus $x=1+3t$ and $y=2-3t$. Eliminating $t$ gives

$$
x+y=3.
$$

This is the general form. A normal vector is $(1,1)$.

Checks:

$$
A=(1,2):\ 1+2=3,\qquad B=(4,-1):\ 4-1=3.
$$
