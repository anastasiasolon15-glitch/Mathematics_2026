# Exercise 14: Contrast: Intersection and Parallelism

The plane is $z=0$, so its normal vector is $k=(0,0,1)$.

For the line from $P=(0,0,1)$ with direction

$$
v_1=(2,1,-1),
$$

the parametric line is

$$
(x,y,z)=(2t,t,1-t).
$$

Since $v_1\cdot k=-1\neq0$, the line is not parallel to the plane. Set $z=0$:

$$
1-t=0,\qquad t=1.
$$

The intersection point is

$$
(2,1,0).
$$

For the direction

$$
v_2=(2,1,0),
$$

we have

$$
v_2\cdot k=0,
$$

so the direction is parallel to the plane. Moreover, the line has $z=1$ for every parameter:

$$
(x,y,z)=(2t,t,1).
$$

Thus it never meets $z=0$. This parallelism can be recognized directly from $v_2\cdot k=0$, without solving an intersection equation.
