# Exercise 11: Plane Containing a Line and a Point

A point on the line is

$$
A=(1,0,2),
$$

and its direction vector is

$$
d=(1,1,-1).
$$

The vector from $A$ to $P=(0,2,1)$ is

$$
AP=P-A=(-1,2,-1).
$$

A normal vector is

$$
d\times AP
=
\begin{vmatrix}
i&j&k\\
1&1&-1\\
-1&2&-1
\end{vmatrix}
=(1,2,3).
$$

Thus the plane through $A$ is

$$
(x-1)+2y+3(z-2)=0,
$$

or

$$
x+2y+3z=7.
$$

Every point on the line has the form $(1+t,t,2-t)$, and

$$
(1+t)+2t+3(2-t)=7.
$$

Also,

$$
0+2(2)+3(1)=7.
$$

Both the line and $P$ lie in the plane.
