# Exercise 7: A Line Relative to a Plane

The line direction is $v=(1,-1,0)$, and the plane normal is $n=(1,1,1)$. Their dot product is

$$
n\cdot v=1-1+0=0.
$$

Thus the line is parallel to the plane.

Test the line point $p=(1,1,0)$:

$$
1+1+0=2.
$$

It satisfies the plane equation, so the entire line lies in the plane.
