### Exercise 1. Solution

**1. Sizes of the matrices**

- $A$ has 2 rows and 3 columns, so $A$ is a $2 \times 3$ matrix.
- $B$ has 3 rows and 2 columns, so $B$ is a $3 \times 2$ matrix.

**2. Entries** (the entry $x_{ij}$ is in row $i$, column $j$)

- $a_{12} = -1$ (row 1, column 2 of $A$)
- $a_{23} = 5$ (row 2, column 3 of $A$)
- $b_{21} = -2$ (row 2, column 1 of $B$)
- $b_{32} = 1$ (row 3, column 2 of $B$)

**3. Row and column vectors**

Second row of $A$:

$$
\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 4 & 5 \end{pmatrix}
$$

First column of $B$:

$$
\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}
$$

**Answers:** $A$ is $2\times 3$, $B$ is $3\times 2$; $a_{12}=-1$, $a_{23}=5$, $b_{21}=-2$, $b_{32}=1$.
